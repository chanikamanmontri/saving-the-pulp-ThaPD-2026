# Saving the Pulp

**Clinical Steps in Managing Deep and Extremely Deep Caries in Young Permanent Teeth**

Lecture notes for the Thai Association of Pediatric Dentistry (ThaPD) Annual Meeting 2026, 26 November 2026.

Assoc. Prof. Chanika Manmontri, DDS, MS, Diplomate ABPD
Division of Pediatric Dentistry, Faculty of Dentistry, Chiang Mai University

**Read online:** https://chanikamanmontri.github.io/saving-the-pulp/

Version 1.0 · Updated 23 Nov 2026

## About

An interactive guide (in Thai, with English technical terms) that follows the seven clinical decisions in managing deep and extremely deep caries in young permanent teeth, from diagnosis to follow-up. It compares six international guidance documents (ESE 2019, AAE 2021, ESE 2023, AAPD 2025, IAPD 2026, EFCD-ESE-ORCA 2026) and shows where they agree and differ. The notes are based on:

Manmontri C, Chompu-inwai P, Chaipattanawan N. Vital pulp therapy in permanent teeth: a comparative analysis of six contemporary international guidance documents with a unified clinical decision framework. *J Clin Pediatr Dent.* 2026;50(6):9-21. https://doi.org/10.22514/jocpd.2026.140

## Disclaimer

These notes support a lecture. They are not a clinical practice guideline. Treatment decisions for each patient rest with the treating dentist. Please consult the original sources in the reference list.
